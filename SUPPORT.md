# Support

## How to Get Help

| Channel | Use for |
|---------|---------|
| [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/kubeopsai/k8s-telemetry-mcp/issues) | Bug reports, feature requests, public questions |
| kubeopsai@gmail.com | Billing questions, private issues, Enterprise support |

## Response Time SLA

| Tier | First Response | Target Resolution |
|------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Standard** ($29/mo) | 5 business days | Best effort |
| **Professional** ($99/mo) | 2 business days | 5 business days |
| **Enterprise** ($299/mo) | 1 business day | 3 business days |

Business hours: **Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00 CET**.

> This is an independently operated product. You will always receive an acknowledgement within the stated first-response window and regular updates until the issue is closed.

## Reporting a Bug

Please include:
1. Server version (`kubectl logs -n monitoring -l app.kubernetes.io/name=k8s-telemetry-mcp | head -5`)
2. Which tool failed and what prompt triggered it
3. The error message returned
4. Your backend (Loki/Prometheus/Tempo, Datadog, or CloudWatch)
5. Your subscription tier

Full SLA details: https://github.com/kubeopsai/k8s-telemetry-mcp/wiki/Support
